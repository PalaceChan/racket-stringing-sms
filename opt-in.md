At racket drop-off, the customer provides a mobile number and is asked:

> May we text you when your racket is ready?

A yes is recorded with the racket order. No ready-for-pickup or status texts are sent without that consent. Consent to receive these messages is separate from any other agreement or service decision.

The messages are limited to the racket order: ready-for-pickup notices and occasional status or delay updates. The customer may reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for help.
