Racket Stringing SMS Notifications is operated by Andres Velazquez.

Racket Stringing SMS Notifications is the name of the messaging program, not a separate business or trade name; the business operates under the owner's own name, Andres Velazquez.

At in-person racket drop-off, the customer provides a mobile number and is read this exact script:

> Can I text you from Racket Stringing SMS Notifications when your racket is ready — usually one to three texts per racket? Message and data rates may apply; you can reply STOP anytime or HELP for help. Privacy and terms are at palacechan.github.io/racket-stringing-sms. It's not required to get your racket strung. Is that a yes?

The script names Racket Stringing SMS Notifications, the program operated by Andres Velazquez; describes ready-for-pickup and status texts; states the frequency as usually one to three texts per racket; states that message and data rates may apply; gives the STOP and HELP replies; points to the Privacy Policy and the Terms and SMS Disclosure published at palacechan.github.io/racket-stringing-sms; states that consent is not required to get a racket strung; and asks for an explicit yes or no.

Only an affirmative yes enrolls the customer. The consent record includes date/time, mobile number, racket order, in-person acquisition method, and campaign. No message is sent before consent. Immediately after enrollment, the business sends this confirmation:

> Racket Stringing SMS Notifications: You're enrolled for racket pickup and status texts, typically 1–3 per racket. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

Enrollment is by this in-person verbal process only; there is no text-to-join keyword.

Message frequency varies, typically 1–3 messages per racket. Msg & data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.
