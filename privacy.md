This privacy policy describes the information used for the Racket Stringing SMS Notifications program operated by an individual racket stringer (sole proprietor).

## Information collected

At drop-off, the service may collect the customer's name, mobile number, and racket or order details needed to identify and complete the stringing service. A record is also kept of whether the customer consented to ready-for-pickup and status texts.

## How information is used and retained

The information is used to perform the racket-stringing service, identify the order, and send the customer-care notifications described on this site. Mobile numbers and consent records are kept while needed to provide the service and document consent; after the order and any ordinary service-record needs are finished, they are removed from active notification records or otherwise handled under the owner's record-retention practice.

## Who has access

Access is limited to the individual stringer and the assistant software used to send notifications on the stringer's behalf. Information is not used for advertising or unrelated messages.

**Mobile information and SMS consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.**

Questions about this policy or the SMS program can be sent to hooxen@agentmail.to.
