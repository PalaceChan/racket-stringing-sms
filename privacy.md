This privacy policy describes the information used for the Racket Stringing SMS Notifications program.

**Registered operator:** Andres Velazquez.

Racket Stringing SMS Notifications is the name of the messaging program, not a separate business or trade name; the business operates under the owner's own name, Andres Velazquez.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

## Information collected

At drop-off, the service may collect the customer's name, mobile number, and racket or order details needed to identify and complete the stringing service. A record is also kept of whether the customer consented to ready-for-pickup and status texts.

## How information is used and retained

The information is used to perform the racket-stringing service, identify the order, and send the customer-care notifications described on this site. Mobile numbers and consent records are kept while needed to provide the service and document consent; after the order and any ordinary service-record needs are finished, they are removed from active notification records or otherwise handled under the owner's record-retention practice.

## SMS consent and choices

At in-person racket drop-off, the customer is asked for explicit yes-or-no consent using this exact spoken script:

> Can I text you from Racket Stringing SMS Notifications when your racket is ready — usually one to three texts per racket? Message and data rates may apply; you can reply STOP anytime or HELP for help. Privacy and terms are at palacechan.github.io/racket-stringing-sms. It's not required to get your racket strung. Is that a yes?

Only an affirmative yes enrolls the customer. The consent record includes the date and time, mobile number, racket order, in-person acquisition method, and this SMS campaign. The program sends ready-for-pickup and occasional order status or delay notices. Message frequency varies, typically 1–3 messages per racket. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Enrollment is by this in-person verbal process only; there is no text-to-join keyword.

## Who has access

Access is limited to the individual stringer and the assistant software used to send notifications on the stringer's behalf. Information is not used for advertising or unrelated messages.

**We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.**

Questions about this policy or the SMS program can be sent to hooxen@agentmail.to.
